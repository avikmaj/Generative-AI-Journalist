# Destruction — Debris, Collapse & Impact FX

## Destruction Has Stages

Nothing instantaneously disappears when destroyed. Every destructive event has a sequence: initial force, structural response, progressive failure, collapse, and aftermath.

---

## Building Collapse

### Controlled Demolition
**Sequence:** Explosive charges fire progressively → structure weakens at base → pancake collapse → massive dust cloud

**Prompt phrase:** `building demolition, progressive collapse from base, floors pancaking downward, massive dust cloud expanding radially, debris rain outside the cloud perimeter`

### Combat-Damage Collapse
**Sequence:** Repeated impacts → structural damage visible → partial collapse → spreading failure

**Prompt phrase:** `combat-damaged structure, [impact type] causing progressive failure, partial walls falling, interior becoming visible, spreading to full collapse`

---

## Glass Destruction

### Window Strike
**Prompt phrase:** `window impact, [projectile/force], glass shattering outward, cascade of glass particles, [slow-mo: individual glass pieces tumbling with reflections]`

### Glass Floor / Ceiling
**Prompt phrase:** `glass surface fracturing under load, crack web spreading from impact point, glass sections falling, [water/material] following through`

---

## Wall and Door Breach

### Explosive Breach
**Prompt phrase:** `explosive wall breach, detonation, wall material fragmenting outward, dust and smoke through the hole, entry immediately after`

### Battering / Forced Entry
**Prompt phrase:** `door/wall under repeated impact, deformation progressive, failure sudden, material fragmenting inward`

---

## Vehicle Destruction

### Crash Impact
**Prompt phrase:** `vehicle impact at [speed], deformation on contact, glass shattering, mechanical components separating, physical laws of inertia visible in aftermath`

### Rollover
**Prompt phrase:** `vehicle rollover, rotation physics correct, roof deformation, glass cascade, tumbling until energy dissipated`

---

## Surface Impacts

### Bullet Impacts on Various Surfaces
- Stone: `chip and dust, ricochet spark possible`
- Concrete: `crater and dust, fragment spray`
- Wood: `splinter and chunk, entry hole visible`
- Metal: `dent and ring, possible penetration, spark`
- Earth/soil: `dirt splash, small crater`

---

## Aftermath Debris

Post-destruction environments have specific visual signatures:
**Prompt phrase:** `destruction aftermath, rubble field, [material composition], fine dust settled on all surfaces, [fire if applicable], structural elements at wrong angles, [scale of devastation]`

---

## Golden Rule

> Destruction is irreversible and has weight. Every fragment has mass; every collapse has a direction. Physics does not pause for dramatic effect — it accelerates it.

# Magic FX — Energy, Portals, Supernatural & Elemental

## Magic Has Rules

Magic in film is only impressive when it has clear rules: a source, a cost, a limit. Without rules, anything can happen and nothing matters. Design the magic system before designing the FX.

---

## Magic System Design Template

```
MAGIC SYSTEM: [Name / IP]
Source:        [where does this power come from?]
Cost:          [what does the user sacrifice or risk?]
Limit:         [what can't this power do?]
Visual signature: [what does it look like?]
Colour:        [dominant colour and meaning]
Sound:         [what does it sound like?]
```

## Energy FX

### Hand / Body Energy

Power radiating from a character's body, hands, eyes.

**Prompt phrase:** `energy radiating from [source — hands/body/eyes], [colour], crackling and pulsing, light cast on surroundings from energy source, particles trailing`

### Energy Beam / Projectile

Directed energy from a source to a target.

**Prompt phrase:** `directed energy beam from [source], [colour], straight trajectory, impact on [surface] — [explosion / absorption / deflection], beam lighting nearby surfaces`

### Energy Shield / Barrier

Protective force field — visible only when struck.

**Prompt phrase:** `energy barrier, [shape — dome/flat plane], visible as [translucent colour] field, impact ripple where force connects, hexagonal or fracture pattern on stress`

## Elemental Magic

### Fire Magic

**Prompt phrase:** `conjured fire, [scale], unnatural movement — controlled by will not physics, [colour — natural orange / arcane blue-white / corrupt black], caster illuminated by their own flames`

### Water / Ice Magic

**Prompt phrase:** `water conjured from air, [scale], crystalline ice forming rapidly, frost patterns expanding, cold light and reflections, ice catching scene light`

### Lightning / Storm Magic

**Prompt phrase:** `lightning called from [sky/hands], crackling arc, branches of electricity, blinding flash, thunder immediate, ozone and ionization feel`

### Earth / Stone Magic

**Prompt phrase:** `ground splitting, stone rising, earth elemental movement, massive scale, rumble and crack, dust and rock fragments`

## Portals

### Creation

**Prompt phrase:** `portal opening, [shape — circular / irregular tear / door-sized], [entry colour — blue / gold / dark rift], edges crackling or defined, [what is visible through it — another location / void / light]`

### Travel Through

**Prompt phrase:** `character stepping through portal, transition effect — [stretched light / dissolve / visual distortion], [what they move from and to]`

### Closing

**Prompt phrase:** `portal collapsing, edges closing inward, [implosion / seal / collapse], final flash as it closes`

## Supernatural Effects

### Possession / Corruption

**Prompt phrase:** `signs of supernatural possession — [eyes changing colour / veins visible / unnatural movement], [character's body resisting or yielding], dark energy visible at extremities`

### Healing / Restoration

**Prompt phrase:** `healing light, [colour], wound closing, restoration visible, [warm glow / crystalline repair / organic regrowth], light fading as healing completes`

### Transformation

**Prompt phrase:** `transformation sequence, [from] to [to], [morphing / shattering and reforming / dissolve], structural change visible, new form emerging`

### Ghostly / Spectral

**Prompt phrase:** `spectral figure, [translucency level], slight temporal offset — movement slightly wrong relative to physics, [colour temperature — cold blue / sickly green / warm gold], interaction with physical world [passes through / creates cold / makes sound]`

## Magic by Visual Colour Reference

| Colour | Common meaning |
|---|---|
| Blue-white | Ice, purity, divine, cold power |
| Gold-amber | Holy, royal, fire of the divine, blessing |
| Deep red | Blood magic, forbidden, passion, corruption |
| Green | Natural magic, corruption/poison, life |
| Purple / violet | Death magic, necromancy, forbidden knowledge |
| Black / dark void | Destruction, void, absolute power or evil |
| White | Transcendence, void at the other extreme |

---

## Golden Rule

> Magic FX must have weight. It must cost something visually — the caster should show effort, the environment should respond, the impact should be physically grounded even when the power is impossible.

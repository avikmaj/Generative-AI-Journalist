# Weather FX — Rain, Storm, Fog, Snow & Atmosphere

## Weather Is Mood

Weather is not background decoration — it is the emotional state of the world. A scene set in rain is a different scene than the same scene set in sunshine. Design weather as a character.

---

## Rain

### Light Rain / Drizzle
Barely visible individual drops. Surfaces wet and reflective. Umbrellas optional.

**Prompt phrase:** `light drizzle, wet surfaces reflecting ambient light, slight mist at edges, cool and grey`

### Heavy Rain
Sheets of water, limited visibility beyond 20–30 meters, surfaces streaming.

**Prompt phrase:** `heavy rainfall, sheets of rain, visibility reduced, ground surface streaming with runoff, rain splashing on all surfaces, characters soaked`

### Storm Rain
Near-zero visibility. Wind drives rain horizontally. Lightning possible.

**Prompt phrase:** `storm rain, rain driven at angle by high wind, near-zero visibility, lightning illuminating in bursts, all surfaces overwhelmed with water`

### Rain on Glass / Window
One of the most atmospheric compositions in cinema.

**Prompt phrase:** `rain on glass, water streaking down exterior surface, [interior character behind glass blurred], reflections in streaming water`

## Fog and Mist

### Light Mist
Atmospheric depth — background elements slightly softened.

**Prompt phrase:** `light atmospheric mist, distance softened, depth cues visible, cool moisture in air`

### Heavy Fog
Visibility 10–30 meters. Objects appear and disappear. Threat hidden.

**Prompt phrase:** `heavy fog, visibility cut to [distance], objects emerge and disappear at threshold, grey-white wall at limits of vision`

### Volumetric Fog
Light shafts made visible through moisture particles.

**Prompt phrase:** `volumetric fog, light shafts visible through moisture, god rays, atmospheric depth with light interaction`

### Ground Fog
Low-lying fog at ankle-to-knee height. Ground disappears.

**Prompt phrase:** `low ground fog, knee-height mist layer, ground obscured, figures appear to float above the fog layer`

---

## Snow

### Light Snow / Flurries
Individual flakes visible, minimal accumulation.

**Prompt phrase:** `light snowfall, individual flakes visible, gentle drift, beginning accumulation on surfaces`

### Heavy Snowfall
Thick accumulation, visibility reduced, surfaces blanketed white.

**Prompt phrase:** `heavy snowfall, thick precipitation, white accumulation on all horizontal surfaces, footsteps visible in new snow, visibility reduced`

### Blizzard
Near-zero visibility, driving wind, survival conditions.

**Prompt phrase:** `blizzard conditions, near-zero visibility, snow driven horizontally, all surfaces obliterated in white, survival conditions`

### Snow Aftermath (Still, Fresh)
One of the most beautiful environmental conditions — silent, still, everything pristine white.

**Prompt phrase:** `post-snowfall, still and silent, fresh white accumulation on all surfaces, low grey sky, sound absorbed by snow`

---

## Wind

### Gentle Breeze
Environmental animation — leaves, hair, light fabric.

**Prompt phrase:** `gentle breeze, leaves rustling, light hair and fabric movement, nothing structural`

### Strong Wind
Environmental disruption — branches moving, dust and debris airborne.

**Prompt phrase:** `strong wind, branches bending, dust and debris airborne, characters' clothing and hair driven [direction], difficult to stand upright in gusts`

### Storm Wind
Physical force. Characters must lean into it.

**Prompt phrase:** `storm-force wind, characters physically pressed by force, debris flying, structural items compromised, howling audio`

---

## Dust and Sand

### Desert Heat Shimmer
Thermal distortion above hot surfaces.

**Prompt phrase:** `desert heat shimmer, thermal distortion above ground surface, wavering visual effect, extreme heat visible`

### Dust Storm (Haboob)
Wall of dust reaching hundreds of meters high, engulfs everything.

**Prompt phrase:** `dust storm wall approaching, brown-orange cloud hundreds of meters high, zero visibility inside, static electricity in the wall`

### Battlefield Dust
Churned by vehicles, explosions, movement.

**Prompt phrase:** `battlefield dust, multiple churning sources, reduced visibility, particles airborne and settling`

---

## Lightning

**Prompt phrase:** `lightning strike, instantaneous white flash illuminating entire scene, [immediate / delayed] thunder, storm atmosphere, brief total visibility followed by darkness`

## Weather by Genre

| Genre | Weather signature |
|---|---|
| Crime / Noir | Night rain, wet streets, reflections |
| War | Mud and rain, dust, smoke |
| Horror | Fog, storm, isolated cold |
| Fantasy | Any extreme — blizzard, tempest, supernatural weather |
| Drama | Weather as emotional mirror — grey for grief, storm for crisis |
| Action | Rain and wind for kinetic energy, fire weather in combat |
| Thriller | Cold, grey, urban wet |

---

## Golden Rule

> Weather changes what a scene means. Rain on a reunion is different from sunshine on a reunion. Design weather as emotional direction.

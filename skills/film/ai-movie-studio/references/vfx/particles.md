# Particles — Dust, Sparks, Atmospherics & Debris

## Particles Build Atmosphere

Individual particles are nearly invisible. Their aggregate effect creates atmosphere, texture, and physical presence. A scene with correctly designed particle behavior feels alive; without it, environments feel sterile and artificial.

---

## Dust

### Ambient Dust Motes
Slow-floating particles in light shafts.
**Prompt phrase:** `ambient dust motes, floating slowly in light shafts, gentle random movement, visible in cross-light, very slow settling`

### Impact Dust
From an explosion, footstep in dry earth, or impact.
**Prompt phrase:** `impact dust, outward burst from source, rapid expansion then gradual drift, heavy particles settle first, fine particles remain suspended`

### Disturbed Dust (Old Building)
When movement disturbs a settled dust layer.
**Prompt phrase:** `disturbed dust layer, rising from [surface] when [action], slow cloud, then gradual settling`

---

## Sparks

### Metal Strike Sparks
**Prompt phrase:** `sparks from metal impact, arc outward from contact point, bright orange-white, short trajectory, fade to dark quickly`

### Electrical Sparks
**Prompt phrase:** `electrical sparks, irregular branching arc, blue-white, rapid and random, accompanied by crackling`

### Grinding Sparks
**Prompt phrase:** `grinding spark shower, continuous stream from contact, waterfall of sparks, direction follows contact angle`

---

## Smoke Particles

### Thin Smoke Trail
**Prompt phrase:** `thin smoke trail from [source], rising slowly, dispersing upward, slight drift with ambient air`

### Heavy Smoke Cloud
**Prompt phrase:** `heavy smoke, billowing cloud, light from above defining structure, dense and opaque, drifting with wind`

---

## Ash and Embers

### Falling Ash
**Prompt phrase:** `ash falling slowly, grey-white particles, extremely light — drifts and eddies rather than falls directly, accumulates on horizontal surfaces`

### Floating Embers
**Prompt phrase:** `embers rising from [fire source], orange-red glowing particles, rising in thermal column, cooling and going dark as they rise`

---

## Water Particles

### Water Spray / Mist
**Prompt phrase:** `water spray mist, fine particles catching light, rainbow potential in direct light, settles as fine droplets`

### Rain Splash Particles
**Prompt phrase:** `rain impact splash, water particles bouncing upward from impact, fine mist rising from heavy rainfall on hard surface`

---

## Golden Rule

> Particles have mass. Even the lightest particles respond to air movement and gravity. Design the source, the direction, the weight, and the settling behavior.

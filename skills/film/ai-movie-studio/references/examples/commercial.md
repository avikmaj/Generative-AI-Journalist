# Commercial — Worked Example

## Example: "UNCHANGED" — Automotive Brand Commercial (60 seconds)

---

## COMMERCIAL BRIEF

```
CLIENT: [Premium automotive brand]
PRODUCT: Full-size SUV
DURATION: 60 seconds
OBJECTIVE: Emotional positioning — this vehicle is for people who have 
           arrived somewhere but haven't forgotten where they started.
TONE: Quiet confidence. Earned, not performed.
VISUAL APPROACH: One man, one drive, dawn. Cities and open country.
TARGET: 35–55, successful, nostalgic for something they can't name.
```

---

## SCRIPT

```
0:00–0:05: OPEN ON: Pre-dawn. A suburban house. Dark windows except one.

0:05–0:10: INTERIOR: Man, 48, preparing for something. 
           Coffee in hand. The kind of face that remembers being younger 
           and wouldn't trade this for it.

0:10–0:20: EXTERIOR: He approaches the vehicle in the driveway. 
           Pre-dawn blue — the world before the day decides what it is.

0:20–0:35: MONTAGE — THE DRIVE:
  Highway at speed, city skyline at dawn.
  Country road, mist, the engine note settling.
  A bridge, water below, the light changing.

0:35–0:45: DESTINATION: He arrives. Somewhere that matters.
           We don't fully see where. He does. That's enough.

0:45–0:55: CU: His face. Something settled in it. The drive did something.

0:55–1:00: TITLE CARD: [Brand]. "Unchanged."
           Hold. Silence.
```

---

## Prompt Package

### Shot 1 — Pre-Dawn House

```
TONE: quiet anticipation — the calm of a person who knows where they're going.
GRADE: blue-hour cool, deep shadow, single warm window lit in the house.
ENVIRONMENT: suburban home exterior, pre-dawn, 4:30 AM, overcast.
CAMERA: wide establishing, static.
LIGHTING: no ambient light except one interior window warm; exterior deep blue.
SFX: silence with slight wind, distant highway in the extreme background.
```

### Shot 5 — The Drive (Country Road)

```
TONE: the specific pleasure of a machine doing what it's made for.
GRADE: transitioning — blue-hour becoming first gold as dawn breaks.
ENVIRONMENT: two-lane country road, mist in low areas, trees either side.
CHARACTER: man at wheel, one hand, relaxed. We see his face through the windshield.
CAMERA: exterior — low angle tracking alongside, 85mm, 
        then interior — dashboard to face, shallow focus.
LIGHTING: natural dawn transition, no artificial sources.
SFX: engine note, road texture, distant birds beginning.
```

---

## Commercial Production Notes

- Commercial grade: warm, premium, never cold or clinical
- No dialogue — the emotion must be image and music only
- The vehicle is present but not hero — the feeling is hero
- 60 seconds of visual argument for a specific emotional state: earned quiet confidence

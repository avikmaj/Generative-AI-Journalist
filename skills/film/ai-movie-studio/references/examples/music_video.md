# Music Video — Complete Worked Example

## Example: "THE WEIGHT OF WATER" — Cinematic Music Video

---

## MUSIC VIDEO BRIEF

```
SONG: "The Weight of Water" (fictional)
ARTIST: [Solo female vocalist]
GENRE: Dark cinematic pop / post-rock
DURATION: 3:45
CONCEPT: A woman returns to the house where she grew up after her mother's death. 
         The house holds what the present cannot.
VISUAL APPROACH: Non-linear — present (cleaning the house) intercutting with 
                 memory (mother alive, the woman as a child).
GRADE: Desaturated present / warm memory — colour is time.
```

---

## CHARACTER LOCKS

```
IDENTITY — ARTIST (present, adult):
Female, early 30s. [visual description — hair, face, clothing for this video].
Present wardrobe: practical — jeans, old sweater, her mother's coat borrowed. 
Colour: grey-blue, functional.

IDENTITY — MOTHER (memory):
Female, 55. Warm face. Lived-in hands. 
Memory wardrobe: an apron over a summer dress, yellow with small flowers.
She is always in warm light. She is summer.
```

---

## SCENE-TO-SONG MAPPING

```
INTRO (0:00–0:30): No vocals. Instrumental only.
Visual: Exterior of the house. Autumn. Artist arrives alone.
Grade: desaturated, blue-cold.

VERSE 1 (0:30–1:00): Vocals enter — quiet, close-mic.
Visual: Artist inside, beginning to pack things. Present tense.
Intercutting to: memory flash — mother in the same kitchen, same light from different angle.

CHORUS 1 (1:00–1:30): Music opens up.
Visual: Artist finds something — [object significant to the relationship].
Memory: the same object, mother's hands holding it, summer light.
The cut between them is the emotional core of the video.

VERSE 2 (1:30–2:00):
Visual: More of the house. Different rooms. The accumulation of absence.
Intercutting memory: different seasons in the same spaces.

CHORUS 2 (2:00–2:30):
Visual: Artist outside, in the garden. Her mother used to be here.
She stands where her mother stood. The camera finds this.

BRIDGE (2:30–3:00): Music drops to minimal — vocalist alone.
Visual: Still frame. Artist, face CU. Something letting go.
No intercutting here. Just her. The camera holds.

FINAL CHORUS (3:00–3:30): Music builds to full.
Visual: Artist and memory artist in the same frame simultaneously — 
        the woman she is standing in the space her mother occupied.
        Not a ghost shot — a deliberate visual metaphor.

OUTRO (3:30–3:45): Music fades. Silence returns.
Visual: The house exterior again. The door closes. The house is alone again.
Final hold. Fade to black.
```

---

## Grade System (Colour as Narrative)

```
PRESENT (desaturated):
Grade: cool blue-grey, reduced saturation, lifted shadows, 
       no warmth allowed in the grade
Time of day: overcast autumn — the light is kind but grey

MEMORY (warm):
Grade: warm amber-gold, saturated, deeper shadows, 
       the world more beautiful than it could have been
Time of day: summer — golden hour quality even when it's midday
The warmth is the memory's argument for itself.

TRANSITION BETWEEN:
The cut between present and memory is the grade cut.
No dissolve — hard cut, and the colour tells you where you are.
```

---

## Lip Sync Direction

```
LIP SYNC SHOTS:
Valid lip-sync shots in this video: held CU and MCU, static camera.
Invalid shots: any camera movement, any wide shot, any cut during a phrase.

Prioritize: the emotional truth of the performance over technical sync.
If lip sync fails on a shot, prefer the image over a garbled sync.
```

---

## Production Notes

- Music video generates approximately 15–20 clips
- The grade system carries the narrative — maintain it absolutely
- The bridge is the emotional centre — maximum stillness here
- Final image (house exterior, door closed) should mirror the opening

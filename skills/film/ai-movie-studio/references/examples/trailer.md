# Trailer — Complete Worked Example

## Example: "THE LAST HANDLER" Official Trailer

*2-minute theatrical trailer for the crime thriller from feature_film.md*

---

## TRAILER BRIEF

```
GOAL: Create anticipation and dread. 
      The audience should leave the trailer asking: "Does he make it?"
TONE: Cold. Precise. The trailer withholds more than it reveals.
MUSIC: Hybrid score — orchestral strings with electronic pulse. 
       Silence at the turn. Single braaam at the title.
FINAL QUESTION: Can Cole protect someone he can't trust with his life?
```

---

## TRAILER PACKAGE — 2 MINUTES

### BEAT 1 — WORLD (0:00–0:18): Cold open. No title. No context.

```
SHOT 1 (0:00–0:06):
Extreme wide — a city at night seen from distance. 
Rain. Just the city. No character.
AUDIO: ambient city rain, no music yet.

SHOT 2 (0:06–0:12):
Street level. Wet pavement. Neon refracted in puddles. 
Someone's feet walking through frame — we don't see who.
AUDIO: footsteps on wet pavement, rain continues.

SHOT 3 (0:12–0:18):
Interior. Cole alone at a window. The shot from Scene 1 of the feature.
He doesn't turn. 
AUDIO: first note of score enters — single low string, barely there.
```

### BEAT 2 — INCITING INCIDENT (0:18–0:35):

```
SHOT 4:
The Control/Cole meeting (from Scene 2). Quick.
CONTROL: "She's asking for you specifically."

SHOT 5:
Cole's face. He processes this. One second.

SHOT 6:
External — a figure being escorted. We see Vera for the first time.

NARRATION (deep, controlled):
"Someone who trusted you once wants to trust you again."

SHOT 7:
Cole's reply from Scene 2 — clipped:
COLE: "She can ask for someone else."

TITLE CARD FLASH: "FROM [STUDIO]"
```

### BEAT 3 — ESCALATION MONTAGE (0:35–1:15):

```
Rapid intercut. Music building. Score adding layers.
Each cut: 2-3 seconds.

[Shot: Cole and Vera moving through a crowd — urgency]
[Shot: CU Vera's face — she's afraid of something specific]
[Shot: A figure watching from distance — we sense the kill team]
[Shot: Cole on phone — something just changed]
[Shot: Running sequence — handheld, tight, unclear geography (intentional)]
[Shot: A door slamming — close quarters]
[Shot: Cole and Vera in a car — she asks something; he doesn't answer]
[Shot: External — a building we haven't seen]
[Shot: CU Cole's hands — they're shaking slightly; first time we see this]

NARRATION:
"Somewhere between what he did and who she is..."

[Shot: Medium Cole looking at something off-screen]
[Shot: The corridor — dark, just the threat implied]

NARRATION:
"...the debt he owes and the one she owes him..."

[Shot: Vera, alone, the expression of someone who has made a decision]
```

### BEAT 4 — THE TURN (1:15–1:22): SILENCE

```
Music cuts to silence.

SHOT: CU on Cole's face — the moment of maximum stakes.
Hold. Three full seconds of silence.

VERA (barely audible, close-mic, almost to herself):
"I didn't think you'd come."

COLE: (long pause — then, flat):
"Neither did I."

Silence.
```

### BEAT 5 — CHAOS MONTAGE (1:22–1:52):

```
Music back — harder now, percussion driving, the score's most aggressive passage.
Cuts at 0.5-1 second.

[Action shots — fragments only, nothing fully revealed]
[The warehouse, glimpsed]
[Cole in the corridor fight — one hit, pulling back]
[Vera running]
[A gun raised — whose? Cut before we see]
[Cole and Vera back to back]
[An explosion glimpsed — distance, context unclear]
[CU Cole's eyes]
[The film's most spectacular visual — the one money shot, full 2 seconds]
[Cut to black]
```

### BEAT 6 — TITLE AND FINAL BEAT (1:52–2:00):

```
BLACK.
Silence.

TITLE CARD: "THE LAST HANDLER"
Hold 2 seconds.

Then — from silence — one final audio beat:
VERA: (quietly, almost amused):
"You were always better at this than you pretend."

Silence.

Then: RELEASE DATE.
```

---

## Trailer Music Direction

```
0:00–0:18:    Near-silence. Single low string. A world at rest (barely).
0:18–0:35:    Score establishes — low pulse, strings.
0:35–1:15:    Building — adding layers, percussion entering mid-montage.
1:15–1:22:    SILENCE. Abrupt. This is the trailer's most powerful moment.
1:22–1:52:    Hard back — maximum drive, all elements.
1:52–2:00:    SILENCE again — let the title breathe.
```

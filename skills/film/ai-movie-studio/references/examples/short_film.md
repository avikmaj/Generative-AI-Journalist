# Short Film — Complete Worked Example

## Example Project: "HOLD"
*A 7-minute drama*

---

## PRODUCTION BIBLE (Short Form)

```
TITLE: Hold
FORMAT: Short film, 7 minutes, 16:9
GENRE: Drama
LOGLINE: A combat veteran and her estranged daughter have 24 hours before 
         the veteran deploys again — and neither knows how to say what needs saying.
THEME: The things love cannot say but cannot stop trying to.
TARGET EMOTION: Quiet grief and the stubborn persistence of connection.
TONE: Intimate. Still. The movie is mostly silences and small movements.
VISUAL STYLE: Photorealistic, warm-to-cool grade shifting with emotional register.
              Natural available light only.
```

---

## CHARACTER LOCKS

```
IDENTITY — MAYA (Veteran, mother):
Female, 42. Military physicality — the body of someone who maintains themselves 
professionally, not vanity. Face warm but cautious — the warmth is still there 
but learned to protect itself. Hair pulled back practical, beginning to grey 
at the temples. In the film: civilian clothes she's slightly uncomfortable in 
— well-worn jeans, olive-green button shirt, sleeves rolled. Work boots.
Posture: upright, occupies space efficiently.

IDENTITY — DANI (Daughter, 19):
Female, 19, her mother's jaw and her father's eyes. 
Hair dark, loose, past shoulder. Layered clothing — she dresses for comfort 
and expression both, oversized grey sweater, dark jeans, canvas shoes. 
Posture: slightly closed — she's used to bracing.
```

---

## SCENE PACKAGE

### Scene 1 — Arrival (0:00–1:30)

```
EMOTIONAL TONE: the particular awkwardness of people who love each other 
                and have forgotten how to show it.

ENVIRONMENT: suburban home exterior, late afternoon, autumn. 
             Leaves on the ground. Overcast. The light is kind but grey.

CAMERA: wide establishing, then medium as Maya approaches the door.
        Static — the camera observes, doesn't comment.

ACTION: Maya at the door. Raised hand to knock. 
        She pauses — a full three seconds — before knocking.
        (This pause is the film's first statement.)

CUT TO: interior. Dani answering. A moment of assessment on both sides.

DIALOGUE (understated, the surface only):
  MAYA: "Flight's tomorrow at six."
  DANI: (steps back to let her in) "I know."

LIGHTING: exterior — overcast, flat, autumnal. 
          Interior — warmer, practical lamps, someone lives here.
AUDIO: wind in leaves, ambient suburb.
```

### Scene 3 — The Kitchen (2:30–4:00)

```
EMOTIONAL TONE: the performance of ordinary things when the ordinary 
                has become unbearable.

ENVIRONMENT: kitchen. Warm practical. A meal being prepared. 
             Everything too careful.

CAMERA: medium, both in frame, then CU alternating.
        The camera is still — it lets the actors carry it.

ACTION: They are making a meal together. This should look practiced 
        and distant simultaneously — they know each other's rhythms 
        and are performing not having them.

DIALOGUE (the surface conversation that carries something else under it):
  DANI: "How long is the deployment?"
  MAYA: "Seven months. Maybe less."
  DANI: (long pause, looking at what she's chopping) "Right."
  (Silence — 8 seconds — that is the scene's most important moment.)
  MAYA: "I made sure the payments are—"
  DANI: "Mom."
  (Another silence. Maya stops. She knows.)

LIGHTING: kitchen warm practicals, 2800K, soft fill from window at grey.
AUDIO: cooking ambient, the specific sounds of this activity.
MUSIC: none. Silence is doing the work.
```

### Scene 5 — The Final Night (5:30–7:00)

```
EMOTIONAL TONE: love after the words have run out — 
                which is the only love that survives anything.

ENVIRONMENT: living room, late. Just the two of them.

CAMERA: wide, then very slow push-in over the scene's duration.
        Static at the beginning. The push-in is so slow it's almost imperceptible 
        until the final moment.

ACTION: They are sitting together. Watching something on television 
        neither is watching. Then: Dani leans her head on Maya's shoulder.
        Maya does not move — for a moment she seems to not breathe.
        Then her hand comes up and rests on Dani's hair. 
        The smallest possible gesture. Everything.

DIALOGUE: none.

LIGHTING: television screen blue-white on their faces, 
          one lamp at far right, warm and distant. 
          The light does the emotional work.

AUDIO: distant television audio, the house at night, their breathing.
MUSIC: piano enters here — single note, sustained. 
       The simplest possible theme. Held until cut to black.

FINAL IMAGE: Maya's hand on Dani's hair. Hold. Fade to black.
```

---

## Why This Works

- Total runtime: 7 minutes / approximately 6 clips
- The film is about what isn't said — the camera design reflects this: static, observational, patient
- The grade shifts from cool exterior to warm interior — the film's emotional movement in colour
- The final image earns its weight because everything before it withheld
- No scene states what the film is about — the audience arrives at the theme themselves

# Lighting — Cinematic Setups & Emotional Design

## Light Is Emotion

Cinematographers say: amateurs light so you can see. Masters light so you can feel. Every light placement is a decision about mood, character psychology, and story tone. There is no neutral light.

---

## The Three-Light Foundation

### Key Light
The dominant, primary light source. Creates the main illumination and most of the shadow.
- Hard key (direct, undiffused) → drama, noir, harsh reality
- Soft key (diffused through large source) → beauty, naturalism, intimacy

### Fill Light
Reduces the shadow cast by the key. Raises the shadow side, softening contrast.
- High fill (strong) → bright, commercial, accessible
- Low fill (weak) → high contrast, dramatic, threatening

### Rim / Back Light
Placed behind the subject, separates them from the background. Creates depth.
- Strong rim → mythic, heroic, backlit against light source
- Subtle rim → depth without drawing attention to itself

## Natural Light Setups

### Golden Hour

**Time:** 30–60 minutes after sunrise or before sunset.

**Quality:** Warm (2000–3500K), directional, long shadows, amber through orange.

**Emotional impact:** Beauty, nostalgia, love, hope, the day's end, something precious and fleeting.

**Scene fit:** Romance, reunion, final moments, journeys beginning or ending, triumph.

**Prompt phrase:** `golden hour lighting, warm amber-orange key from low angle, long raking shadows, volumetric golden haze, 2500K colour temperature`

---

### Blue Hour

**Time:** 20–30 minutes after sunset or before sunrise.

**Quality:** Cool blue (5000–7000K), diffused, no direct shadows, sky as large soft source.

**Emotional impact:** Mystery, melancholy, transition, the boundary between day and night, isolation.

**Scene fit:** Crime, surveillance, loneliness, departure, introspection.

**Prompt phrase:** `blue hour lighting, desaturated cool blue ambient, sky as primary source, no hard shadows, 6500K, transitional light`

---

### Moonlight

**Quality:** Cool silver-blue (6000–8000K), directional but soft, deep shadows, silver highlights.

**Emotional impact:** Mystery, danger, romance with darkness, supernatural, dreams.

**Scene fit:** Horror, fantasy, crime at night, romantic outdoor scenes, myth.

**Prompt phrase:** `moonlight, cool silver-blue key from above, deep shadows, silver rim on surfaces, 7000K, quiet and dangerous`

---

### Overcast Daylight

**Quality:** Flat, even, diffused (5500–6000K). No directional shadows.

**Emotional impact:** Depression, flatness, the world drained of contrast, grey reality.

**Scene fit:** Aftermath, grief, clinical reality, post-conflict, mundane horror.

**Prompt phrase:** `overcast flat light, diffused even illumination, no directional shadows, grey-white sky ambient, 5600K`

## Atmospheric Setups

### Volumetric Light (God Rays)

**Description:** Light made visible as it travels through dust, fog, or smoke particles.

**Emotional impact:** Transcendence, divinity, hope arriving, scale of the world, sacred spaces.

**Prompt phrase:** `volumetric light rays, light made visible through [dust/smoke/mist/fog], shafts of illumination, atmosphere participates in the lighting`

---

### Fog and Mist

**Description:** Ambient diffusion of the scene. Reduces contrast at distance, creates atmospheric depth.

**Emotional impact:** Mystery, threat hidden, the world concealing something, dream-like unreality.

**Prompt phrase:** `fog atmosphere, light diffused into mist, visibility decreases with distance, hidden depth, [cold/warm] fog colour`

---

### Fire Light

**Description:** Warm, flickering, low-key. Primary source shifts with flame movement. 1500–2000K.

**Emotional impact:** Primal, dangerous, warmth in darkness, ancient, survival, campfire intimacy.

**Prompt phrase:** `firelight, warm flickering source from below, 1700K orange-amber, dancing shadows, darkness recedes at edges`

---

### Candle / Torch Light

**Description:** Single-point warm practical, very low key, heavy shadow falloff.

**Emotional impact:** Vulnerability, ancient settings, survival, the fragility of light in darkness.

**Prompt phrase:** `candlelight, single warm point source, dramatic shadow falloff, [medieval/intimate/dangerous], practical flicker`

---

## Artificial / Stylized Setups

### Neon Night

**Description:** City-at-night with multiple coloured neon practicals. Mixed temperature chaos.

**Emotional impact:** Corruption, energy, alienation, the hyperreal urban night, cyberpunk.

**Prompt phrase:** `neon night lighting, mixed coloured neon practicals [red/pink/cyan/purple], wet reflective surfaces, deep shadows between sources`

---

### Noir Low-Key

**Description:** Single hard key, minimal fill, heavy shadow. High contrast. Classic Venetian blind shadow patterns.

**Emotional impact:** Danger, moral ambiguity, crime, secrets, the darkness inside people.

**Prompt phrase:** `noir lighting, single hard key, near-zero fill, deep shadow covering [half/majority] of subject, high contrast, [Venetian blind shadow pattern if applicable]`

---

### Interrogation / Practical Lamp

**Description:** Single bare bulb or hanging lamp over a hard surface. Cold institutional ambience.

**Emotional impact:** Power, vulnerability, exposure, truth being demanded.

**Prompt phrase:** `interrogation lighting, single overhead practical, [bare bulb / hanging lamp], subject exposed below, surrounding shadow, cold institutional`

---

### Hospital / Fluorescent

**Description:** Overhead fluorescent, flat and greenish, no shadows, clinical.

**Emotional impact:** Sickness, institutional control, the clinical coldness of systems, death made administrative.

**Prompt phrase:** `fluorescent overhead lighting, flat clinical illumination, slight green cast, institutional cold, no shadows`

---

### Lightning / Storm

**Description:** Brief hard white flash, total darkness between. Environment strobing.

**Emotional impact:** Chaos, revelation in darkness, violence of nature, the jump-cut of light and dark.

**Prompt phrase:** `lightning storm lighting, white strobe flash illuminating scene, darkness between flashes, rain wet surfaces reflecting light, chaos`

## Advanced Lighting Techniques

### Rembrandt Lighting

One hard key at 45°, positioned high. The shadow from the nose connects to the shadow from the cheekbone, creating a small triangle of light on the shadow-side cheek.

**Emotional impact:** Gravitas, moral weight, the complexity of a person. Used for portraits of characters with depth.

**Prompt phrase:** `Rembrandt lighting, key at 45 degrees, nose-shadow triangle on shadow cheek, balanced drama`

---

### Split Lighting

Key directly to one side. Exactly half the face lit, half in shadow.

**Emotional impact:** Duality, internal conflict, the character is two people. Villains, characters at moral crossroads.

**Prompt phrase:** `split lighting, face exactly half-lit, sharp shadow line bisecting the face, duality`

---

### Backlight / Silhouette

Subject illuminated only from behind. Face and front in shadow.

**Emotional impact:** Mystery, power of the unknown, the figure as archetype, threat revealed only in outline.

**Prompt phrase:** `backlit subject, [bright source behind], face in shadow, rim of light around subject edges, mystery and silhouette`

---

### Motivated Light

Every light source in the scene has a named, visible justification.

**Rule:** State the source. The lighting behaviour follows from it.

```
"The only light comes from the television screen at frame left, 
 casting cold blue-white light across the left side of his face, 
 leaving the right in near-total darkness."
 
"Three candles on the table. Their pooled warmth reaches to the 
 edge of the room and no further. Beyond: darkness."
```

| Kelvin | Quality | Source | Emotion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1700–2000K | Deep orange | Flame, fire | Primal, survival, danger |
| 2700–3000K | Warm white | Incandescent | Home, comfort, safety |
| 3200K | Warm studio | Tungsten lights | Interior drama |
| 4000K | Neutral warm | Soft studio | Commercial, accessible |
| 5500–5600K | Daylight neutral | Overcast, midday | Natural, clinical |
| 6500K | Cool daylight | Blue hour, clouds | Melancholy, modern |
| 7000–9000K | Cold blue | Moonlight, deep shadow | Fear, night, alienation |

## Lighting by Genre

| Genre | Signature Lighting |
|---|---|
| Crime / Noir | Low-key, hard shadows, practicals, high contrast |
| Horror | Near-darkness, single practical, underlighting, unexpected sources |
| Action / War | High contrast, motivated naturalism, explosions as practicals |
| Romance | Golden hour, soft wrap, warm fill, candlelight |
| Thriller | Cold blue, fluorescent, exposure / vulnerability |
| Fantasy | Volumetric, saturated colour, fire and torchlight |
| Sci-Fi | Cool blue-white, neon accent, hard edge practicals |
| Drama | Soft naturalistic, motivated window light, golden hour |
| Epic / Mythology | God rays, volumetric, sacred glow, fire spectacle |

## Lighting Prompt Template

```
LIGHTING:
  Time/natural source: [golden hour / night / overcast / interior]
  Key light: [direction, quality (hard/soft), colour temperature]
  Fill light: [ratio — heavy fill / minimal fill / no fill]
  Rim/back: [present / absent / strong / subtle]
  Practicals: [any visible sources — fire, neon, screen, lamp]
  Atmosphere: [fog / dust / smoke / clean air]
  Volumetrics: [god rays / none / light smoke]
  Shadow quality: [hard-edged / soft / diffused]
  Dominant colour: [warm / cold / neutral / mixed]
  Emotional register: [one word — danger / beauty / isolation / power / etc.]
```

---

## Golden Rule

> State the source. Let everything follow. A motivated light that behaves consistently is more convincing than a perfectly graded but sourceless image.

## Prompt Approach

Runway Gen-4 introduced character reference consistency features. Use reference images to lock character identity, then direct motion and scene in text.

**Effective phrasing:**
- Motion-forward language: describe what moves and how
- Environment described as atmosphere
- Style in emotional/aesthetic terms
- Action as a continuous movement sequence

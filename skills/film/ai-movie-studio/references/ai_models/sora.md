---

## Prompt Approach

Sora responds well to natural language, narrative descriptions. Write as a director briefing a crew: describe the scene, the action, and the intended feeling in clear prose.

**Structure:** Scene description → Character action → Camera note → Mood/style

**Effective phrasing:**
- "A wide shot of..." (shot types work)
- "The camera slowly pushes in as..." (camera movement in narrative form)
- "Cinematic, shallow depth of field, golden hour lighting..."
- "The scene should feel..." (emotional register)

---

## Sora Strengths
- Long coherence — characters maintain identity over many seconds
- Complex physical environments
- Multi-character interaction
- Time-of-day lighting fidelity

## Example Prompt Structure

```
[Scene]: [environment description, time of day, weather]
[Characters]: [identity description for each present character]
[Action]: [what happens, sequenced]
[Camera]: [shot type, movement, lens feel]
[Style]: [grade, film stock feel, cinematic quality]
[Mood]: [emotional register]
```

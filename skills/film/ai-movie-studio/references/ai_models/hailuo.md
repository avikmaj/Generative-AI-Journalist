---

## Prompt Approach

Hailuo responds well to motion-first descriptions. Lead with what is happening physically, then environment and style.

**Effective phrasing:**
- Action verbs early in the prompt
- Physical specificity in movement description
- Environment in ambient/atmospheric terms
- Cinematic style noted at end

---

## Hailuo Strengths
- Natural human motion quality
- Smooth cinematic movement
- Expression and subtle acting quality

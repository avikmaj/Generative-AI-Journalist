## Prompt Approach

Pika works well with clear, direct language. Describe the image state, then describe the motion. Particularly effective for animating still images.

**Effective phrasing:**
- Start with visual scene as if describing a photograph
- Then describe what moves: "camera slowly pushes in, leaves rustle, character turns head"
- Style in simple terms: "cinematic, film grain, shallow focus"

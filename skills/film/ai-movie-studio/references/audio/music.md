# Music — Film Scoring, Leitmotifs & Genre Templates

## Music Is Subtext

A film score does not describe what's happening on screen. It tells the audience how to feel about it. The image shows the event; the music delivers the emotional instruction. When image and music agree, the emotion is doubled. When they contrast, the dissonance is the meaning.

---

## Leitmotifs

A leitmotif is a recurring musical theme associated with a character, place, relationship, or idea. It evolves over the film to reflect how that element changes.

**Design principles:**
- Keep it short — 4–8 notes is sufficient
- Make it melodically distinctive enough to survive transformation
- Introduce it when the element it represents first appears at full significance
- Transform it as the story demands: invert it for the dark version, slow it for grief, accelerate it for action, harmonize it for triumph

**Leitmotif design template:**
```
LEITMOTIF: [Character/concept name]
Core phrase: [melodic description — e.g. "ascending minor third, settling to a fifth"]
Instrument: [primary instrument when theme is at full statement]
Emotional register: [what it communicates at its base statement]
Dark version: [how it transforms under threat or corruption]
Triumph version: [how it transforms at resolution]
First appearance: [when it first plays in full]
```

## Orchestral Building Blocks

### Strings

**Function:** Emotion's carrier. Strings can move from warmth to anguish to terror without leaving the section.

| String texture | Emotional register |
|---|---|
| Long sustained legato | Grief, longing, beauty, reverence |
| Short staccato pizzicato | Tension, suspicion, quiet threat |
| Fast tremolo | Anxiety, fear building, unstable world |
| High sustained harmonics | Supernatural, transcendence, void |
| Driving ostinato | Momentum, urgency, pursuit |
| Solo cello | Grief, loneliness, the individual against the world |
| Solo violin high | Anguish, love, beauty on the edge of breaking |

### Brass

**Function:** Power, declaration, war, triumph.

| Brass texture | Use |
|---|---|
| Full brass chorale | Victory, epic revelation, arrival at the goal |
| Low brass drone | Threat, size, inevitability, the villain approaches |
| Brassy fanfare | Heroic entry, triumph, military |
| Muted brass | Sinister, dark jazz, sophisticated menace |
| Solo French horn | Nobility, adventure, the hero's theme |
| Tuba low pedal | Oppression, immovable force |

### Woodwinds

**Function:** Colour, character, pastoral, ethnic flavor.

| Woodwind | Emotional register |
|---|---|
| Solo flute, high | Lightness, spiritual, pastoral |
| Oboe solo | Pastoral, longing, sweetness with ache |
| Clarinet | Character, comedy or pathos depending on register |
| Bassoon low | Comedy, menace, the grotesque |
| Pan flute | World music, nature, ancient |

### Percussion

**Function:** Drive, time, physical impact.

| Percussion | Use |
|---|---|
| Taiko drums | Epic, war, power, ancient force |
| Orchestral snare roll | Military, tension building |
| Timpani hits | Punctuation, impact, inevitability |
| Hi-hat ostinato | Tension, contemporary, action |
| Silence after big drum hit | The weight of the moment |

## Ethnic and World Music Elements

### Indian Classical

Instruments: sitar, tabla, bansuri flute, sarangi, mridangam.

**Emotional register:** Ancient, spiritual, mythic, temporal depth, the subcontinent's vast history.

**Prompt phrase:** `Indian classical elements, [sitar/tabla] carrying the lead, rhythmic cycle of 12 beats, ancient and spiritual, mythological register`

### Japanese

Instruments: koto, shakuhachi, taiko, shamisen.

**Emotional register:** Precise, disciplined, meditative, honour, the weight of tradition, cherry blossom transience.

**Prompt phrase:** `Japanese classical elements, koto melodic line, shakuhachi breath and space, taiko punctuation`

### Middle Eastern

Instruments: oud, qanun, darbuka, ney.

**Emotional register:** Ancient, melodically ornate, spiritual tension, desert, the weight of civilizations.

**Prompt phrase:** `Middle Eastern elements, oud carrying melody, microtonal ornaments, darbuka rhythm, ancient and vivid`

### Celtic / Northern European

Instruments: fiddle, uilleann pipes, bodhrán, tin whistle.

**Emotional register:** Folk heroism, the land, ancient struggle, community, melancholy and joy intertwined.

## Hybrid / Electronic Score

Combines orchestral elements with electronic processing and synthesis.

**Common techniques:**
- **Braaam:** Deep, processed brass sound — dread, reveal, the modern blockbuster threat
- **Reverse reverb:** Audio time-reversed — supernatural, the world going backward
- **Granular texture:** Orchestral sounds processed into atmosphere — haunting, disorienting
- **Pulse bass:** Sub-frequency rhythmic pulse — body-level urgency, action, tension
- **Choir processed beyond recognition:** Voices as texture — transcendence, alien, horror

**Prompt phrase:** `hybrid score, orchestral strings with electronic processing, deep bass pulse, [emotional register], modern epic`

## Genre Score Templates

### Action / War

```
SCORE: Driving orchestral action, brass punches on impact, strings in fast ostinato, 
       taiko and orchestral percussion, tempo 140–160 BPM, building to full orchestra 
       at climax, silence after peak impact
```

### Horror

```
SCORE: Minimal — silence and ambient texture dominant, stabs on scare moments, 
       strings in extreme register (high harmonics / low tremolo), no melody that 
       offers comfort, dissonant cluster chords, processed choir texture
```

### Thriller / Crime

```
SCORE: Low pulse, minimal melodic content, jazz-influenced in places (muted brass, 
       walking bass), percussion-driven in chase sequences, silence used as tension, 
       tempo accelerates with plot urgency
```

### Fantasy / Epic

```
SCORE: Full orchestra with choir, thematic development across the runtime, 
       hero leitmotif fully stated at climax, ethnic elements reflecting world design, 
       strings carry emotion, brass carry power, choir at mythic moments
```

### Drama / Emotional

```
SCORE: Piano and solo strings, minimal orchestral support, space given to dialogue, 
       underscore does not explain the emotion — it holds space for it, 
       silence in the most important moments
```

### Sci-Fi / Space

```
SCORE: Hybrid — processed orchestra with synthesized elements, spacious mix with 
       reverb suggesting vastness, electronic pulse for technology sequences, 
       choir for awe moments, cold and warm in balance
```

### Romance

```
SCORE: Warm strings carrying melody, piano, gentle brass at peak moments, 
       no hard percussion, long melodic lines, breathing room, 
       the score is in love with both characters
```

## Score Timing and Film Integration

### Score Entry Points

| Story moment | Score behavior |
|---|---|
| Scene begins quietly | Score enters under action — low, subtle, establishing tone |
| Tension building | Score grows in density and dissonance |
| Action sequence start | Percussion and driving ostinato come in, energy peaks |
| Emotional revelation | Score may reduce to single instrument — piano or solo strings |
| Silence for impact | Score drops out entirely — the scene breathes alone |
| Climax | Full orchestra, all themes, maximum dynamic |
| Aftermath | Score is muted, lower, the world settling |
| Resolution | Theme restated in final form — triumph or grief version |

### The Music Edit

Music has its own rhythm that either agrees with the edit or fights it. Use music cuts to reinforce story cuts:
- **Hard cut to silence:** Impact, shock, the world stopping
- **Music playing through cut:** Continuity, the flow of time
- **New music cue on cut:** Reset — a new section of the story beginning

## Practical Score Direction for AI Generation

When specifying music in a generation prompt:

```
MUSIC:
  Genre: [orchestral / hybrid / electronic / ethnic / minimal]
  Tempo: [BPM or relative — slow / moderate / driving / urgent]
  Dominant instrument: [what carries the lead line]
  Emotional function: [what the score is doing to the audience — not what's happening on screen]
  Dynamic: [soft underscore / building / full statement / silence]
  Theme reference: [if a leitmotif — state which character/idea it belongs to and its current emotional state]
```

---

## Golden Rule

> The score does not illustrate the image. It tells the audience what to feel about it. Design the emotional instruction, not the description.

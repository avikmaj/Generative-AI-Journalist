# Mythology Prompts — Ancient Gods, Epics & Sacred Worlds

## Mythology Operates at Maximum Scale

Mythological storytelling deals with the largest forces that exist: divine will, cosmic order, fate, the relationship between mortals and gods. Every design choice must reflect that scale — environments are vast, consequences are eternal, characters represent more than themselves.

---

## Mythological Storytelling Principles

1. **Characters represent forces, not just people.** A hero is not just a warrior — they embody human aspiration against divine power.
2. **The world is alive and participatory.** Weather, animals, celestial events are not backdrop — they respond to and reflect the story.
3. **Fate and prophecy shape structure.** The audience often knows the end; the power is in watching it arrive.
4. **Scale is always mythic.** Human beings are small against the forces arrayed around them. This smallness makes their courage meaningful.

## World Mythologies — Visual Reference

### Hindu / Vedic Mythology

```
VISUAL SIGNATURE:
  Environments: vast temple complexes, sacred mountains (Meru), cosmic ocean, 
                jungle primordial, celestial realms (Swarga)
  Colour: deep golds, rich reds and saffrons, blue of the divine, white of purity
  Light: volumetric divine glow — the gods bring their own luminescence
  Architecture: carved stone columns, lotus motifs, mandala geometry, towering gopuram
  Figures: divine scale — gods shown larger than mortals, multiple limbs possible for deities
  Sacred objects: lotus, trident (trishula), discus (chakra), mace (gada), conch (shankha)
  Animals: divine vahanas — eagle (Garuda), bull (Nandi), peacock, tiger, white elephant
```

**Prompt formula:**
```
Ancient Vedic world, [specific location — celestial realm / sacred mountain / primordial ocean].
[Time: cosmic time — not morning or evening but the age of the gods].
Architecture: massive carved stone, [style details], age beyond counting.
Light: divine and sourceless — the gods illuminate from within.
Colour: [gold and deep red / cosmic blue / saffron and white].
Scale: human figures are small in this space by design — the world was built for greater beings.
VFX: divine aura [colour] surrounding [deity/sacred object], lotus particles, 
     sacred fire, cosmic energy manifestation.
```

### Greek / Roman Mythology

```
VISUAL SIGNATURE:
  Environments: marble temples, Mount Olympus above clouds, the Underworld, 
                Mediterranean seas, heroic landscapes
  Colour: white marble, gold accents, Mediterranean blue sky, deep shadow
  Architecture: Doric/Ionic/Corinthian columns, pediments, friezes, open-air temples
  Scale: heroic — figures large, muscular, idealized
  Dramatic light: Mediterranean high sun / thunderstorm for Zeus / torch and shadow for Hades
```

### Norse Mythology

```
VISUAL SIGNATURE:
  Environments: frozen mountains, dark primordial forests, Asgard's gold halls, 
                Yggdrasil (the World Tree), the cold grey sea
  Colour: grey-blue cold, gold of Asgard, dark iron and fur
  Architecture: Viking longhouse scale up to divine — carved wood and runic stone
  Weather: wind, snow, storm — the sky is always a participant
  Figures: warriors of heroic build, divine figures vast
```

### Egyptian Mythology

```
VISUAL SIGNATURE:
  Environments: desert, Nile, massive temple complexes, the Duat (underworld)
  Colour: golden sand, deep blue of night sky, hieroglyph colours — red, blue, gold, green
  Architecture: massive pylons, obelisks, hypostyle halls, sphinx forms
  Figures: gods shown in animal-human hybrid forms (falcon-head, jackal-head, etc.)
  Light: harsh desert sun / torchlit interior / the specific light of the afterlife — cold and blue
```

## Deity / Divine Figure Prompt Template

```
DIVINE FIGURE: [deity name / description if unnamed]
DIVINE SCALE: [shown at mortal scale for intimate scene / 
               towering over landscape for power display]
APPEARANCE: [specific divine attributes — multiple arms / divine weapons / animal aspect]
AURA: [colour and quality of divine power surrounding them]
LIGHT: [self-illuminating quality — the god brings light, not reflects it]
WARDROBE: [divine garments — specific to mythology and deity]
MOVEMENT: [gods do not move like mortals — deliberate, each step considered, 
            or instantaneous — there, then somewhere else]
EXPRESSION: [divine authority — not human emotion, but the face of a force 
             that has watched civilizations rise and fall]
```

## The Mortal vs Divine Scale Shot

One of mythology's defining visual moments — a mortal figure against a divine one:

```
COMPOSITION: mortal in extreme foreground (small, specific, human-scale detail)
             deity in background (vast, elemental, the world itself in their posture)
LIGHTING: mortal lit by normal sources / deity self-illuminating, light radiating outward
CAMERA: low angle looking up at deity, mortal between camera and divine figure
EMOTION: awe, terror, reverence — the mortal's face holds all three
SCALE: the deity should be 10-50x the mortal's size in the frame
```

## Epic Battle — Mythological Scale

```
SCALE: armies of mortal warriors + divine/demonic forces.
TERRAIN: [mythic landscape — cosmic battlefield / divine realm invaded / 
           primordial chaos].
DIVINE INTERVENTION: [a deity acts — describe how this looks at mythic scale.
                       Lightning from above / ocean rising / earth splitting].
CHARACTER: one mortal protagonist we follow through cosmic chaos.
CAMERA: [extreme wide to establish mythic scale / 
          tight on character to preserve human stakes amid divine war].
VFX: [divine energy, weapon manifestations, environmental effects of divine power].
AUDIO: [the sound of divine combat — beyond the physics of mortal warfare].
```

## Golden Rule

> Mythology is the story of what it means to be human, told through the lens of what is beyond human. Every divine element exists to illuminate the mortal at the story's center.

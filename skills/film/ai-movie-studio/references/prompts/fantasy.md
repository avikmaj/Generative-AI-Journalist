# Fantasy Prompts — Epic, Magic & Mythic Worlds

## Fantasy Demands Specific Rules

The more impossible a world is, the more specific its rules must be. Fantasy without clear internal logic is noise. Fantasy with clear internal logic is myth.

---

## Fantasy World Lock Template

```
WORLD: [Name]
ERA: [fantasy medieval / ancient mythic / dark ages / other]
COSMOLOGY: [how the world is structured — multiple realms / single world with magic / etc.]
MAGIC SYSTEM: [source, rules, cost, limits — from vfx/magic.md]
FACTIONS: [major powers, their visual signatures]
AESTHETIC: [visual design language — dark and gritty / high fantasy bright / ancient mythic]
COLOUR PALETTE: [dominant world colours, accent colours for magic/power]
ARCHITECTURE: [stone and timber / carved into living rock / grown / arcane construction]
CREATURES: [what exists in this world beyond humans]
HISTORY: [what major events shaped this world's current state]
```

## Epic Fantasy Prompt Templates

### The Hero's Emergence

```
EMOTIONAL TONE: the birth of legend — something is beginning that cannot be undone.
ENVIRONMENT: [sweeping mythic landscape — mountain summit / ancient ruin / battlefield edge].
LIGHTING: [golden hour or dramatic overcast — the world acknowledging this moment].
CHARACTER: [the hero at their moment of commitment or power awakening].
CAMERA: low angle — the hero becomes large against the sky. Slow rise as they rise.
VFX: [subtle power manifestation appropriate to the magic system].
AUDIO: orchestral swell — the hero's theme at full statement for the first time.
COMPOSITION: hero centered or rule-of-thirds, sky dominant, environment vast behind them.
```

### The Battle Charge

```
SCALE: [hundreds / thousands of warriors].
FORMATION: [shield wall / cavalry charge / mixed forces].
TERRAIN: [open field / mountain pass / gate siege].
LIGHTING: [dramatic overcast / golden hour raking light / fire from siege machines].
CAMERA SEQUENCE:
  Wide: the armies facing each other — scale established.
  Low tracking: the charge beginning — feet and hooves.
  Medium character: our protagonist in the charge — fear and determination.
  Wide chaos: the lines meeting — collision of forces.
VFX: [dust / fire arrows / magic if applicable / siege weapon impacts].
AUDIO: war drums building / commands shouted / the roar of voices / the impact of forces meeting.
```

### The Ancient/Forbidden Place

```
ENVIRONMENT: [ruins of prior civilization / sacred location / dark sanctum / mythic threshold].
AGE: [this place predates the current world — everything is ancient].
CONDITION: [maintained by supernatural means / slowly reclaiming by nature / frozen in time].
LIGHTING: [sourceless ambient glow / shafts of light through broken ceiling / 
            practical torches catching writing on walls].
ATMOSPHERE: [thick — dust particles / mist / something in the air].
CHARACTER: [small against the scale of this place — it was built for something larger].
CAMERA: [wide to show scale / slow movement — reverence and danger].
AUDIO: [silence broken only by echoes / wind / water / ancient mechanisms activating].
```

## Creature Prompts

### Dragon

```
CREATURE: dragon, [scale — building-sized / city-scale / island-scale].
BUILD: [massive, heavy, built for power — the weight of a geological event].
WINGS: [span relative to body — wider than it is long, for flight physics].
SURFACE: [scales: [colour] with [lighter/darker] underbelly, 
           each scale catching light individually].
MOVEMENT: [flight: powerful slow wingbeats, each pushing air downward visibly /
            walking: deliberate, ground impact reverberating].
FIRE/BREATH: [fire: white-hot at source, orange-red exterior, billowing forward].
EYES: [intelligent, ancient, the gaze of something that has watched centuries].
```

### Ancient Creature / Entity

```
CREATURE: [name / type — elder god / giant / ancient beast].
SCALE: [dwarfs the environment].
MOVEMENT: [gravity of mass — slow, inevitable, each step felt before it is heard].
APPEARANCE: [specific — not generic monster. What is specific and wrong about it?].
PRESENCE: [the environment responds to it — birds flee, water disturbs, temperature changes].
```

## Fantasy Visual Style Reference

| Sub-genre | Visual signature |
|---|---|
| High Fantasy | Bright, saturated, noble colours, clean lines, heroic scale |
| Dark Fantasy | Muted, grey-green, firelight, shadow dominant, grim textures |
| Epic Mythology | Gold and deep colour, volumetric light, divine scale |
| Urban Fantasy | Modern world + magic = contrast between mundane and impossible |
| Sword and Sorcery | Gritty, practical, magic is rare and costly |
| Ancient Mythology | Stone and bronze, fire and smoke, the gods are real |

## Golden Rule

> Fantasy earns its wonder by earning its darkness first. The beauty is only beautiful because something threatens it.

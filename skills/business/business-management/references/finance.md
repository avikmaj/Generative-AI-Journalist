## Financial Management (Book 37)

**Three financial statements:**
- Income Statement: Revenue − Expenses = Net Income (profitability)
- Balance Sheet: Assets = Liabilities + Equity (financial position)
- Cash Flow Statement: Operating + Investing + Financing (cash health)

**Key financial ratios:**

| Ratio | Formula | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Gross margin | (Revenue − COGS) / Revenue | Product profitability |
| Net margin | Net income / Revenue | Overall profitability |
| Current ratio | Current assets / Current liabilities | Short-term liquidity |
| ROE | Net income / Shareholder equity | Return to shareholders |
| ROI | (Gain − Cost) / Cost | Return on investment |
| Debt-to-equity | Total debt / Total equity | Leverage level |
| Burn rate | Monthly cash outflow | Runway (startups) |

**Cash flow management priorities:**
1. Know your cash position weekly (not monthly)
2. Accelerate receivables, delay payables (within ethics)
3. Maintain 3–6 months operating expenses as reserve
4. Separate operating, tax, and growth cash pools

**Break-even analysis:**
Break-even = Fixed Costs / (Price − Variable Cost per Unit)

## Financial Crisis & Housing Crisis (Books 39–40)

**2008 Financial Crisis key lessons:**
- Systemic risk: interconnected institutions = contagion
- Leverage amplifies both gains and losses
- Liquidity can evaporate faster than solvency
- Risk models fail in tail events
- Regulatory gaps enable systemic risk build-up

**Business crisis response framework:**
1. Stabilize (stop the bleeding — cash, headcount, suppliers)
2. Assess (what is real vs fear)
3. Restructure (right-size to sustainable base)
4. Rebuild (growth from stable foundation)

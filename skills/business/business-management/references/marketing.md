## Business Management Bible — Books 29–35

## Marketing Frameworks (Book 29)

**STP Framework:**
Segmentation → Targeting → Positioning

**Segmentation criteria:**
- Demographic: age, income, education, gender
- Geographic: location, climate, urban/rural
- Psychographic: values, lifestyle, personality
- Behavioural: usage, loyalty, purchase occasion

**4Ps / 7Ps Marketing Mix:**
Product · Price · Place · Promotion
(+) People · Process · Physical evidence (services)

**Marketing funnel metrics:**
Awareness (reach/impressions) → Consideration (CTR/engagement) →
Conversion (leads/sales) → Retention (churn/repeat) → Advocacy (NPS/referrals)

---

## Brand Development (Book 30)

**Brand Pyramid (bottom to top):**
Functional attributes → Emotional benefits → Brand personality →
Brand values → Brand essence (one word/phrase)

**Brand consistency rule:** Every touchpoint should feel like
it came from the same source. Visual + verbal + behavioral.

**Brand architecture models:**
- Monolithic: one master brand (Apple)
- Endorsed: sub-brands under parent (Marriott/Courtyard)
- Freestanding: independent brands (P&G)

## Content Creation Blueprint (Book 31)

**Content strategy pillars:**
- Hero content: big campaigns, wide reach (1–2× year)
- Hub content: regular series, community building (weekly)
- Help content: SEO-driven, problem-solving (always-on)

**Content creation process:**
Audience insight → Topic clusters → Format selection →
Production → Distribution → Measurement → Iteration

## Customer Journey Blueprint (Book 34)

**Customer Journey stages:**
Awareness → Consideration → Decision → Retention → Advocacy

**Journey map components:**
- Stage, customer goal, touchpoint, emotion, pain points,
  opportunities, internal owner, KPI per stage

**Net Promoter Score (NPS):**
Promoters (9–10) minus Detractors (0–6) = NPS
Above 50 = excellent · 0–50 = good · Below 0 = needs urgent action

## Customer Relationship Management (Book 35)

**CRM lifecycle:**
Acquire → Onboard → Engage → Retain → Win-back

**Customer retention economics:**
5% increase in retention = 25–95% increase in profit
(Bain & Company research cited in Bible)

Acquiring new customer = 5–25× more expensive than retaining one.

**CRM best practices:**
- Segment customers by value (Pareto: 20% of customers = 80% of revenue)
- Personalize communications at scale
- Measure customer health score (usage + support tickets + engagement)
- Proactive outreach before churn signals appear

## Storytelling (Book 32)

**Story structure for business:**
Context (where we are) → Conflict (the problem/tension) →
Resolution (how we solve it) → Call to action

**Pixar storytelling formula:**
Once upon a time... Every day... Until one day... Because of that...
Until finally... And ever since then...

**Business storytelling applications:**
Investor pitch · Sales presentation · Change communication ·
Employee engagement · Brand narrative · Case studies

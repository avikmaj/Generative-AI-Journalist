## Business Management Bible — Books 10–16

## Strategic Management (Book 10)

**Strategy hierarchy:**
Corporate strategy → Business unit strategy → Functional strategy

**Strategic planning process:**
Mission → Vision → Values → Environmental scan (PESTLE + SWOT) →
Strategic options → Selection → Implementation → Review

**SWOT Analysis:**
```
Internal:  Strengths / Weaknesses
External:  Opportunities / Threats
```

**PESTLE:**
Political · Economic · Social · Technological · Legal · Environmental

---

## Strategic Thinking Blueprint (Book 11)

**Strategic thinking vs strategic planning:**
- Planning = structured process with defined outcomes
- Thinking = ongoing habit of seeing patterns and possibilities

**Five disciplines of strategic thinkers:**
1. See the whole system (not just your function)
2. Reframe problems before solving them
3. Absorb diverse perspectives
4. Balance present and future tension
5. Translate insight into action

## Competitive Advantage (Book 13)

**Porter's Five Forces:**
1. Threat of new entrants
2. Bargaining power of suppliers
3. Bargaining power of buyers
4. Threat of substitutes
5. Industry rivalry

**Generic strategies (Porter):**
- Cost leadership: lowest cost in market
- Differentiation: unique value buyers will pay premium for
- Focus: cost leadership or differentiation in a niche

**VRIO Framework (resource-based view):**
Valuable → Rare → Inimitable → Organized to capture
All four = sustained competitive advantage

**Blue Ocean Strategy:**
Eliminate-Reduce-Raise-Create grid
Move from red ocean (competition) to blue ocean (new market space)

## Decision Models (Book 14)

| Model | Use case |
|---|---|
| Decision matrix | Multiple options, multiple criteria |
| Cost-benefit analysis | Financial trade-off evaluation |
| Pareto (80/20) | Prioritization of effort |
| OODA Loop | Fast-cycle decisions under uncertainty |
| Pre-mortem | Risk identification before commitment |
| Eisenhower Matrix | Urgency vs importance prioritization |
| First principles | Strip assumptions, rebuild from base truths |
| BATNA | Negotiation walk-away point |

**Decision quality checklist:**
☐ Problem correctly framed?
☐ Sufficient alternatives considered?
☐ Key uncertainties identified?
☐ Trade-offs explicitly acknowledged?
☐ Decision reversible or irreversible?
☐ Second-order effects considered?

## Founder Frameworks (Book 15)

**Early-stage decision filters:**
- Does this make the product better or not?
- Does this get us closer to customers or not?
- Does this preserve runway or not?

**Founder mindset shifts (startup → scale):**
Individual contributor → team builder → org designer → culture keeper

**Key founder frameworks:**
- Product-market fit signal: 40% rule (Sean Ellis test)
- Jobs to Be Done: customers hire products to do a job
- The Innovator's Dilemma: disruption comes from below
- Crossing the Chasm: early adopters ≠ early majority

## Risk Management (Book 16)

**Risk management process:**
Identify → Assess (probability × impact) → Prioritize →
Mitigate → Monitor → Report

**Risk response strategies:**
- Avoid: eliminate the activity causing the risk
- Transfer: insurance, contracts, outsourcing
- Mitigate: reduce probability or impact
- Accept: conscious decision to absorb the risk

**Risk register fields:**
Risk ID · Description · Category · Probability (1-5) ·
Impact (1-5) · Risk score (P×I) · Owner · Response · Status

**Risk categories:**
Strategic · Operational · Financial · Compliance ·
Reputational · Technology · People

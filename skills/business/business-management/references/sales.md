## Sales Strategies (Book 26)

**Sales funnel stages:**
Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Intent → Evaluation → Purchase

**Consultative selling process:**
1. Research prospect before contact
2. Open with insight (not product pitch)
3. Diagnose before prescribing
4. Present solution to confirmed problem
5. Handle objections with evidence
6. Close with clear next step
7. Follow up to ensure value delivered

**SPIN Selling (Rackham):**
Situation → Problem → Implication → Need-payoff questions

**Challenger Sale:**
Teach · Tailor · Take control
Best for complex B2B: challenge customer's assumptions first.

**Sales metrics to track:**
Conversion rate per stage · Average deal size · Sales cycle length ·
Win/loss ratio · Customer acquisition cost (CAC) · Pipeline coverage (3×)

## Negotiation (Books 27–28)

**Harvard Negotiation Principles:**
- Separate people from the problem
- Focus on interests not positions
- Invent options for mutual gain
- Insist on objective criteria

**BATNA (Best Alternative to Negotiated Agreement):**
Know your walk-away before entering any negotiation.
Strengthen BATNA before negotiating — it is your real leverage.

**Negotiation planning (Gameplan Blueprint):**
1. Define your BATNA and ZOPA (Zone of Possible Agreement)
2. Research the other side's interests and constraints
3. Identify all variables (not just price)
4. Anchor first when you have data to support it
5. Use silence as a tool — never fill it

## Pricing Strategies (Book 36)

| Strategy | Mechanism | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Cost-plus | Cost + margin % | Manufacturing, commodities |
| Value-based | Price = perceived value | Premium, B2B SaaS |
| Competitive | Match/beat market price | Commodity markets |
| Penetration | Low price to gain share | New market entry |
| Skimming | High price, lower over time | Innovation, early adopters |
| Freemium | Free tier + paid upgrade | Software, platforms |
| Dynamic | Price varies by demand/time | Airlines, hotels, surge |
| Bundle | Package of products/services | Increase average order value |

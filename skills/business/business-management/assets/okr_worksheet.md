## Rules

- Two to three objectives. More than three means no priority.
- Three to five key results per objective, each a **number moving from a baseline to a target**.
  "Launch the portal" is a task, not a key result; "portal handles 60% of tickets, up from 0%" is.
- Record the baseline. Without it, the target is unfalsifiable.
- Confidence scored 1–10 at set-time and updated weekly. A KR at confidence 10 was not ambitious.
- Separate committed KRs from aspirational ones, and never tie compensation to aspirational ones.

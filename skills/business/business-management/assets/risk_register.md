## Rules

- Write risks as `condition → consequence`. "Supplier risk" is not a risk; "if our sole PCB supplier
  misses the June window, launch slips a quarter" is.
- Every row has a named owner and a due date. Unowned rows are deleted or escalated, not carried.
- Response is one of exactly four: avoid, mitigate, transfer, accept.
- Track residual exposure after mitigation. Sign-off accepts residual risk consciously.

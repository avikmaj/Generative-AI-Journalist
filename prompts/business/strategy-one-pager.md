# Strategy one-pager

Expected skill: `business-management`. Expected mode: `[BIZ]`.

```text
Draft a one-page strategy.

Business / unit: <NAME AND WHAT IT DOES>
Time horizon: <QUARTERS OR YEARS>
Known constraints: <BUDGET, HEADCOUNT, REGULATORY, TECHNICAL>
Evidence available: <ATTACH OR SUMMARISE>

Use the one_page_strategy asset structure. Return:
1. The filled one-pager.
2. An assumption table: each assumption, how load-bearing it is, and the cheapest test of it.
3. The two strategies you rejected and why.

Any market size, growth rate, competitor claim or benchmark must be sourced or labelled UNVERIFIED.
Financial illustrations must be labelled SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE. Do not present a plausible number as a
researched one.
```

## Why it is shaped this way

Strategy documents are where invented numbers do the most damage. Separating assumptions from
findings, and demanding the rejected options, exposes whether a real choice was made.

# Decision brief

Expected skill: `business-management`. Expected mode: `[BIZ]`.

```text
Write a decision brief.

Decision to be made: <THE ACTUAL QUESTION>
Decision owner and date needed: <WHO, WHEN>
Options on the table: <LIST, OR "propose them">
What we know: <ATTACH OR SUMMARISE>

Use the decision_brief asset. Return the brief plus:
1. An options table: option, cost, benefit, risk, reversibility.
2. What would have to be true for each option to be right.
3. The recommendation, under a Recommendation heading, with the confidence and what would change it.
4. What is still unknown, and whether it is worth the delay to find out.

Keep facts and judgement visibly separate. Do not recommend the option that needs least explanation.
```

## Why it is shaped this way

Reversibility and "what would have to be true" are the two columns that most often flip a decision,
and both are usually missing from AI-drafted briefs.

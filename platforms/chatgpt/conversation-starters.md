# Conversation starters

Four is the ChatGPT maximum. These are chosen to exercise both families and both depths.

1. `Turn this spec section into a verification plan with coverage mapping.`
2. `Review my UVM agent structure and flag reuse and config-DB problems.`
3. `Design a RAG pipeline over my OUTSKILL notes and tell me how you would evaluate it.`
4. `Harden this prompt: list its failure modes, then rewrite it with a contract.`
